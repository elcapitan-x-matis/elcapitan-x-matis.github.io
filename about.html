About Me

Full Profile

Knowledgeable in computing technologies and systems development, problem-solving skills and proven experience in creating and designing software in a test-driven environment.

I am currently an ICT Support Officer — App & Dev. at PNG Customs Service & ASYCUDA World, where I provide second-level technical support for desktops, networks, and applications. My role covers Office 365 and SharePoint platform management, SQL database management and reporting from the ASYCUDA World system, Active Directory and Azure AD account management, and maintenance and development of the PNG Customs Service website.

Beyond my core role, I have delivered freelance web applications including a Staff Loan Management System and a Student Management System, both built with PHP, MySQL, and JavaScript. In 2022, I received the Telikom PNG Award at Divine Word University for Best Final Year Project — a System Architecture Proposal Design for a Student Management System for the National Polytechnical Institute of Technology.
